These terms govern every service sold by Bassin Digital: website design and redesign, search optimisation, social media management, artificial intelligence services and maintenance.

They prevail over the client's own purchasing terms unless agreed otherwise in writing. Bassin Digital may amend them at any time; the version that applies is the one in force on the day of the order, and you receive a copy with your quote.

Who these services are for. Bassin Digital works mainly with professional clients — freelancers, tradespeople, shopkeepers and companies acting in the course of their business — and also accepts orders from private individuals. Services are sold and invoiced from Georgia, and may be sold anywhere in the world.

If you are a consumer, meaning you are ordering for purposes outside any trade or profession, the mandatory provisions of the law of your country of habitual residence apply and prevail over any conflicting clause in these terms. The clauses specific to you are flagged throughout the document.

Every project is covered by a written quote, valid for 30 days. The online configurator gives an immediate estimate based on the options you choose; that estimate becomes contractual once it is carried over into the final quote accepted by both parties.

An order is firm when all three of the following are met: the quote is accepted in writing (email is sufficient), these terms are accepted, and the deposit has been received.

Anything not listed in the quote is covered by a separate additional quote.

04Prices and payment

Prices are stated in euros. As Bassin Digital is not registered for VAT (article 2), invoices are issued without VAT.

  • Business client established in the European Union: the reverse charge applies. You declare and pay VAT in your own country at the applicable rate, and you give us your EU VAT number when you place the order.
  • Business client outside the European Union: invoiced without VAT, each party bearing its own local tax obligations.
  • Private individuals: the price quoted is the final price. No VAT and no additional charge is added by Bassin Digital at invoicing.

Any duties, taxes, withholding taxes or bank charges payable in your country remain your responsibility and may not be deducted from the amounts due to Bassin Digital.

  • 30% deposit on ordering, non-refundable once work has started (see article 16).
  • 70% balance on delivery, before the site goes live.
  • For projects above €5,000, a three-instalment schedule may be agreed in the quote.

Subscriptions — CARE maintenance, AI services — are invoiced in advance, monthly or annually depending on the plan chosen.

Payments are made by bank transfer, to the European IBAN shown on the invoice. Late payment

If you are a business established in the European Union, these amounts may not fall below the minimums set by Directive 2011/7/EU on late payment as transposed into your national law. If you are a consumer, the €40 fixed recovery fee does not apply and the interest rate charged may not exceed what the law of your country of residence permits. Bassin Digital may suspend work in progress, including hosting, after a reminder has gone unanswered for 15 days.

16Cancellation and termination

If the client cancels after work has started, the deposit is retained by Bassin Digital and the work already done is invoiced in proportion to its progress. This does not affect a consumer client's right of withdrawal, described in article 17.

In the event of a serious breach by either party — non-payment, no response for more than 60 days, failure to meet an essential obligation — the other party may terminate the contract 15 days after a formal notice has gone unheeded.

If a project is suspended at your request for more than 90 days, Bassin Digital may close it and invoice the progress made.
