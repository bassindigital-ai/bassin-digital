05Cookies

The site uses two categories of tracker.

Strictly necessary trackers — they keep the site working technically and remember your choice about audience measurement. They do not require your consent, in line with the rules on trackers applicable in your country of residence.

Audience measurement (Google Analytics 4) — it tells us how many people visit the site and which pages interest them. You can also set your browser to accept or refuse cookies at any time.
