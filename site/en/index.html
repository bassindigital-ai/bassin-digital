Why those three, and not TikTok or LinkedIn?

Because that is where your clients are. For a tradesperson, a restaurant or a shop around the Bassin d'Arcachon, Instagram, Facebook and the Google profile carry almost everything that gets you found and chosen. If your business lives elsewhere — LinkedIn for B2B, TikTok for a younger crowd — we add it, and we discuss it at the first meeting. Three channels genuinely kept up beat a fifth one left to rot.

So where does AI come in?

It saves us time on layout and on the first draft of the copy. It does not decide for you and it never publishes on its own. What makes your clients choose you — your work, your place, the way you speak — does not automate, and we do not pretend otherwise.

What about paid advertising?

We can talk about it, but rarely straight away. Paying to send people to an empty page is wasted budget. A few months of steady presence first; then we look together at whether ads make sense for you, and at what budget.

Frequently asked questions

What we get asked before we start

Your project

How much does a custom showcase website cost?

From €1,500 for the NEO package: 5 pages, custom design, contact form, GDPR compliance and one year of hosting included. The online configurator gives you an exact price in a few minutes; a 30% deposit books the project.

Why not a €30-a-month subscription or a €500 website?

Because they are not the same thing. With a subscription you rent a website: you pay for as long as you use it, it looks like thousands of others, and the day you leave you leave empty-handed. Here you buy yours: built for your trade, it belongs to you, source code included. If a €500 website is enough for your project, tell us plainly — we would rather say so than oversell you.

How long does it take to deliver my website?

It depends on the package and on how quickly we exchange: a showcase website is usually delivered within a few weeks once we have your content; a business or bespoke website takes longer. The timeline is set at the start and written into the quote.

I have neither copy nor photos. Is that a problem?

No, and it is very common. We write the copy from an interview with you — you know your trade, we know how to write it for the web and for Google. For images we use your existing photos, quality stock libraries, or we organise a shoot (included in the PREMIUM package).

What if I don't like the result?

You never discover the website at the end. You approve a design mock-up first, then the developed website, and each stage includes two rounds of feedback. Nothing goes live without your approval.

Do I own my website?

Yes, entirely: the code, the design, the content and the domain name are yours. No subscription is required: CARE maintenance is optional. While you are on CARE, hosting, the domain and the certificate are covered by the plan; if you stop it, we hand over the website and every access, and those costs become yours.

Artificial intelligence

What is an AI phone assistant?

A voice assistant that answers your calls 24/7 in your company's name, gives your opening hours and prices, books appointments and sends you a summary by SMS or email. You listen to a sample before deciding. Setup €1,490, then from €99 a month.

Won't it sound robotic?

Listen and judge for yourself: we play you a sample before any commitment, with your wording and your answers. And the assistant introduces itself as one — we never let your customers believe they are talking to a human. The real question is not whether it is perfect, it is whether it beats a phone ringing into the void.

I know nothing about AI. Is it complicated to run?

You have nothing to install, nothing to learn and nothing to monitor. We configure it, connect it to your tools, test it with you, and you get a simple report every month. That is exactly the work you are handing over.

Is €99 a month worth it?

Ask it the other way round: what does one extra customer bring you, and how many calls or enquiries slip past you each week because you are out on site? If the answer is none, the subscription is useless — and we will tell you so. Otherwise it pays for itself quickly. And if you would rather start small, social media and Google profile management begins at €190 a month with no setup fee.

Working together

You work remotely — how does that work in practice?

A first video or phone call to understand your business, then a shared workspace where you follow progress and drop your content. Approvals happen in writing, milestones over video. Our clients are in France, in Europe and beyond, in French or English; invoicing is in euros.

Is my data — and my customers' data — protected?

Yes. Websites are delivered GDPR-compliant: consent before any audience tracking, form data used only to answer you, secure hosting. When we process data on your behalf — form, chatbot, automation — we act as a processor, under a written agreement.

What happens after the first year of hosting?

Two options. The CARE plan: hosting, domain name, backups, updates and 2 hours of changes a month, all included (€49 a month as a launch offer). Or you take over: we hand over the website, the domain name and every access, and you pay for hosting and the domain directly — expect a few tens of euros a year.
